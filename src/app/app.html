Our Heritage

Welcome to
TURKISH RESTAURANT

Rooted in the timeless banquet culture of Istanbul and Gaziantep, Turkish Restaurant offers an unparalleled fine dining experience in Nishatabad, Faisalabad. We combine genuine charcoal-fired Mangal grilling techniques with the warmest Turkish hospitality.

Our master grill ustas select only the finest cuts of 100% Halal grass-fed mutton and chicken, seasoned with authentic Aleppo peppers, wild sumac, and oregano. Every dish—from our fragrant slow-simmered stews to our crispy stone-baked pide—is a tribute to the rich gastronomy of Anatolia.

Honoring Ottoman Culinary Heritage

Turkish cuisine is one of the world's most storied food cultures—celebrating harmony between Mediterranean freshness and Central Asian charcoal grilling traditions. In Nishatabad, Faisalabad, Turkish Restaurant was conceived to honor this legacy without compromise.

Our marinades begin with hand-crushed Urfa and Maraş peppers, roasted garlic, and pure Aegean cold-pressed olive oil. We cook exclusively over glowing oak wood and charcoal, providing each skewer with that unmistakable aromatic charred tenderness.
